# 🎂 Midnight Crimson Birthday Wishes Web Experience

An interactive, responsive single-page birthday celebration website designed with a dramatic **Crimson Glow & Midnight** aesthetic (deep obsidian black, dark wine, and glowing neon ruby/crimson effects).

---

## ✨ Features

- **Chapter I: Interactive Animated Cake Cutting**
  - Multi-tiered cake with dripping glaze, berries, candles, and flickering neon flames.
  - Step 1: "Make a Wish & Blow Candles" with extinguishing flame and smoke puff animations.
  - Step 2: "Cut Cake" with cyber-knife slice animation and piece separation.
  - Celebration: Metallic confetti cannon blast and 40+ glowing helium balloons that float upward at brisk speed (each can be clicked to pop with mini sparkle bursts!).
  
- **Chapter II: The Crimson Envelope & Typewriter Letter**
  - 3D velvet envelope with an interactive glowing wax seal that breaks and unfolds.
  - Real-time computer typewriter animation with a glowing cursor and typing sound effects.
  - "Show Full Letter" skip button.
  - Pinned retro Polaroid photo cards with neon glows.

- **Chapter III: Friendship Trivia & Playful Quiz**
  - Multi-question interactive trivia with dynamic progress tracking.
  - Immediate visual feedback (emerald celebration / playful shake animations).
  - Trophy celebration card upon completion.

- **Chapter IV: Journey Memory Timeline & "Words for Me" Email Box**
  - Alternating milestone timeline with glowing nodes and photo lightbox modal.
  - Interactive "Words for Me" response box with mood selector (`🥰 Loved`, `🥳 Hyped`, `😂 Laughing`, etc.).
  - Sends directly to your inbox via Formspree API (or pre-fills your email client as fallback).

- **🎵 Audio Engine & Atmosphere**
  - Built-in Web Audio API synthesizer for dreamy ambient background melodies and sound effects (candles blow, chimes, pops, typewriter ticks).
  - Ambient particle canvas with floating crimson embers.
  - Full chapter-locked progression system to ensure a step-by-step storytelling experience.

## 🛠️ Personalization

All personal details, questions, memories, and settings can be easily customized in **`config.js`**:

- `friendName`: Her name
- `friendNickname`: Her nickname
- `senderName`: Your name
- `formspreeEndpoint`: Your Formspree form ID to receive her email responses
- `yourEmail`: Fallback email address
- `musicPath`: Optional custom background MP3 file path
- `letter`: Salutation, paragraphs, and closing message
- `quiz`: Questions, options, correct answers, and feedback messages
- `timeline`: Milestone dates, titles, descriptions, and photo URLs
